    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Bountiful, UT

    How NAD+ Works in Bountiful

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to move electrons while making ATP, and enzymes like sirtuins and PARPs burn through it while repairing DNA and handling stress. Levels decline with age. Injected under the skin, NAD+ skips digestion entirely, but researchers are still working out how much of it reaches muscle, brain and other tissue.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Bountiful

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. Pushing the plunger over about a minute cuts down on flushing.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. A wave of warmth, some queasiness or brief chest tightness right after the shot is common, and it typically passes in a few minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Bountiful

    Flushing leads the list. Injection site redness, stinging and soreness, along with nausea, lightheadedness and headache, are also common. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Bountiful

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. IV NAD+ puts more in at once, though sessions often last hours and the discomfort during the drip is a common complaint. The nasal spray is needle-free. Oral precursors like NMN and nicotinamide riboside are supplements that help the body make its own NAD+.
