    How GLP-1 Microdosing Works in Herriman

    Microdosing is a dosing strategy, not a separate drug. It means taking semaglutide or tirzepatide at doses lower than the standard labeled schedule, or increasing the dose more slowly, while the medicine still works on the same gut hormone receptors. Others notice little change. Because brand pens come in fixed doses, low-dose plans often use compounded medication, which is not FDA-approved.

    Who GLP-1 Microdosing Is For

    Three groups ask about it most. The typical candidates had trouble with nausea on standard doses, prefer a gentler start or are trying to hold onto weight they already lost. It is not a shortcut. Claims that microdosing treats inflammation or aging are not backed by strong evidence.

    Low-Dose Schedule in Herriman

    There is no single standard schedule. Providers often start below the usual first dose, such as a fraction of the 0.25 mg semaglutide or 2.5 mg tirzepatide starting dose, and increase in smaller steps than the label calls for. Most plans stay weekly. Never adjust the dose on your own.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Early weeks vary a lot between people. Some notice less food noise and earlier fullness within the first two weeks, while others notice almost nothing at a very low dose and need a small increase to feel an effect. Nausea is usually milder. Expect modest, gradual change.

    Side Effects in Herriman

    Lower doses reduce side effects but do not erase them. The same stomach symptoms appear at low doses, including nausea, reflux, constipation and diarrhea, mostly after each small step up. The boxed warning remains. Pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and the thyroid C-cell tumor warning from rodent studies apply at any dose.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening is the same as for full doses. Your provider reviews your BMI, weight history, conditions, medications and any past GLP-1 use, including the highest dose you reached and why you stopped. Thyroid cancer history rules it out. Past pancreatitis or an eating disorder needs a longer conversation.

    Microdosing Compared With Standard GLP-1 Treatment in Herriman

    Standard dosing has the trial data. Wegovy and Zepbound at full doses were tested in large clinical trials that showed average weight loss of about 15 to 20 percent, while microdosing has not been studied at that scale. Expect smaller average results. Maintenance is a common use case.
