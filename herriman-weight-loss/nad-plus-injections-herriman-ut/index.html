    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Herriman, UT

    How NAD+ Works in Herriman

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Older tissue tends to have less. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want steadier energy, clearer thinking or better recovery. Some add NAD+ during GLP-1 weight loss to support how they feel while eating less. NAD+ does not treat disease or cause weight loss, and pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or serious liver or kidney disease usually rule it out.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Herriman

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Use the belly or thigh with a fine needle. A slow injection makes a real difference.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. A wave of warmth, some queasiness or brief chest tightness right after the shot is common, and it typically passes in a few minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Doses change based on your notes.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Herriman

    Flushing leads the list. Other frequent ones are stinging at the site, nausea, headache and feeling lightheaded. Too much too quickly can cause chest pressure, cramping or palpitations, which typically ease with a smaller dose and a slower push. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and supplements first. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Some cases get a metabolic panel. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Herriman

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. IV NAD+ puts more in at once, though sessions often last hours and the discomfort during the drip is a common complaint. NAD+ nasal spray avoids needles at a smaller dose. Oral precursors like NMN and nicotinamide riboside are supplements that help the body make its own NAD+.
