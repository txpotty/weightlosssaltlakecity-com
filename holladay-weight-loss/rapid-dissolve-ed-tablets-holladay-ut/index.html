    How Rapid Dissolve Tablets Work in Holladay

    Most rapid dissolve ED tablets contain sildenafil, tadalafil or occasionally both. These PDE5 inhibitors relax the smooth muscle in penile blood vessels so blood flows in more easily when you are aroused, exactly as the standard tablets do. You place it under your tongue. Some medication may absorb through the lining of the mouth, and the rest is swallowed with saliva. Arousal is still required.

    Who Rapid Dissolve Tablets Are For

    The usual fit is a man with ED who dislikes swallowing pills or wants something discreet that needs no water.     Dosing and Timing in Holladay

    Rapid dissolve tablets are taken as needed, usually 15 to 60 minutes before sex depending on the formula. Let it sit under your tongue until it is gone, typically one to three minutes, without chewing or swallowing it whole. Hold off on eating or drinking until then. One dose per 24 hours. Strength depends on the active ingredient.

    Side Effects in Holladay

    Side effects follow the active ingredient. Sildenafil and tadalafil formulas both commonly cause headache, flushing, congestion and indigestion, with tadalafil adding back aches for some men. Some men feel mild mouth irritation. An erection lasting over four hours or sudden vision or hearing loss needs emergency care. Rare, but serious.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule these tablets out, including nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers. Riociguat is also excluded. A heart attack or stroke in the past six months, unstable chest pain, uncontrolled blood pressure or severe liver or kidney disease all need careful review and may make the tablets unsafe. Alpha blockers like tamsulosin and drugs like ketoconazole or ritonavir raise drug levels. Food allergies get asked about too.

    Rapid Dissolve Tablets Compared to Other Options in Holladay

    Compounded rapid dissolve tablets are not FDA-approved. FDA-approved generic tablets use the same drugs and carry decades of research, so many providers try them first. Troches take longer to dissolve. A troche sits between the cheek and gum for 10 to 20 minutes. Daily chewables offer steady low-dose coverage instead.
