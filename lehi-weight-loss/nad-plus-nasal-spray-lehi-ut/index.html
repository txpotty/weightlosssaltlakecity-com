    How NAD+ Nasal Spray Works in Lehi, UT

    How NAD+ Nasal Spray Works in Lehi

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. Cells use it to convert nutrients into ATP, and it supports enzymes involved in DNA repair, with levels that tend to decline as people age. Absorption happens in the nose. Part of each spray is absorbed through the vessel-rich lining, skipping digestion, but the total is lower than with injections and brain delivery remains uncertain.

    Who NAD+ Nasal Spray Is Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, focus and healthy aging who prefer to avoid needles are the usual candidates. Some use it to test NAD+ first, and others switch to it after finishing injections. It does not treat disease. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or chronic sinus problems may rule it out.

    NAD+ Nasal Spray Dosing and Schedule in Lehi

    Once or twice daily, mornings first. Many protocols begin with a single spray per nostril in the morning, then increase the count or add a midday dose after a week or two. Avoid evening use. Keep the bottle in the refrigerator and prime the pump with a spray or two into the air if you have not used it in a few days.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Expect some stinging early. A mild burn or an odd taste at the back of the throat is common, and some people report a gentle lift in energy or focus within one to two weeks while others notice little. Over a month, steadier energy is the most common report. Doses change based on your notes.

    NAD+ Nasal Spray Side Effects in Lehi

    Nasal irritation is the most common. Expect some sneezing, a drippy nose or a bitter taste, and a few people get headaches or a brief flush. Nosebleeds are uncommon but can happen with frequent use, especially in dry air or during winter heating season, so a humidifier and aiming the nozzle away from the center of the nose help. Report persistent bleeding.

    Eligibility and Screening

    A provider reviews your history, medications and nasal health first. You will be asked about pregnancy, cancer history, chronic sinusitis, nasal polyps, frequent nosebleeds and any steroid, decongestant or antihistamine sprays you use. Bloodwork is uncommon here. List supplements like NMN too.

    NAD+ Nasal Spray Compared to Injections in Lehi

    Injections deliver more. Subcutaneous NAD+ shots give larger, more predictable doses, and IV drips deliver the most at once but can take two to four hours with more discomfort during the infusion. Convenience is the spray's advantage. Oral NMN and nicotinamide riboside work differently, as building blocks the body uses to make its own NAD+.
