    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in South Jordan, UT

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in South Jordan

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. DHT, made from testosterone, gradually miniaturizes follicles in genetically sensitive men, starting at the temples or crown. Finasteride lowers DHT, and minoxidil supports growth directly. Used together, they usually beat either one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause, but thyroid problems, low iron, a recent pregnancy, crash dieting and major stress can all cause shedding that looks similar. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Off-label oral options, such as spironolactone, are added when appropriate.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in South Jordan

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Minoxidil, FDA-approved as a topical, keeps follicles in the growth phase longer so strands come in thicker. The pairing is the standard. Some men use a compounded topical spray that combines both, which is not FDA-approved but may reduce whole-body exposure to finasteride.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. Approved for prostate enlargement, it is used off-label for hair, usually after finasteride falls short. It lingers. Because it stays in the body for months, providers screen more carefully. Minoxidil is added for growth.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in South Jordan

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. A compounded minoxidil solution with GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, is one option, with minoxidil doing the main work and GHK-Cu adding early, mostly lab-based support for scalp and follicle health. It is compounded. It is not FDA-approved. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Minoxidil is the usual first step for women.

    What to Expect Over Time in South Jordan

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Early shedding on minoxidil is common and usually temporary. Stopping treatment lets the loss resume.
