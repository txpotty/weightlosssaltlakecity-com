    How Sildenafil Works in Taylorsville

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. PDE5 breaks down cGMP, and cGMP is what relaxes the blood vessels in the penis, so with the enzyme blocked, blood flow during arousal improves. Arousal is still required. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. It makes it easier to get an erection and keep it firm enough for sex.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. It works whether the cause is blood flow, nerves, stress or some mix. Diabetes, high cholesterol and treated high blood pressure are common in men who respond, and each deserves its own follow-up. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. That is usually men who want a longer window.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Taylorsville

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Tablets come in 25, 50 and 100 mg, with 50 mg as the usual starting point. No more than one dose in any 24 hour period. A heavy or high-fat meal can delay it by an hour or so, which is why many men take it on a light stomach. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need a few tries to get the timing and the meal situation right. A fair trial is four to eight separate attempts at a given dose, because first-time nerves and a couple of drinks can blunt the effect early on. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Jot down the dose, how long before sex you took it and how it went. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Taylorsville

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. A temporary blue tinge or light sensitivity in vision happens to some men, usually at 100 mg. Dizziness is more likely with alcohol, with blood pressure medication and in men who stand up quickly after taking a 100 mg dose. Sudden vision loss, sudden hearing loss or an erection lasting more than four hours needs emergency care. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. That includes nitroglycerin tablets or spray, isosorbide and recreational poppers, because the combination can drop blood pressure to dangerous levels. The pulmonary hypertension drug riociguat is excluded too. Questions cover recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain that comes on at rest and serious liver or kidney problems. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Taylorsville

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, or can be taken every day at a low dose. Compounded chewables, rapid dissolve tablets and troches offer different formats, but those compounded versions are not FDA-approved. Generic sildenafil is FDA-approved and is chemically the same as Viagra, so it is often where providers start for men who want a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
