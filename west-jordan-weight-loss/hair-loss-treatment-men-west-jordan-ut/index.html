    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in West Jordan, UT

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in West Jordan

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. In genetically sensitive men, DHT gradually miniaturizes follicles, starting at the temples or crown and moving inward. Finasteride lowers DHT, which slows or stops that shrinking. Minoxidil, on its own path, helps follicles grow longer and thicker.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. It works best when there is still visible hair in the thinning areas, because follicles that have been bald for years rarely respond to any medicine. Earlier is better. Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss needs an in-person exam instead. The provider checks your photos for the typical pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in West Jordan

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Minoxidil comes as a topical used once or twice a day, or as a low-dose off-label tablet. A compounded combination spray is applied once a day. Dutasteride, when used, is a daily capsule.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. The early shed is usually a sign follicles are cycling, and it fades. Around months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening. Fuller results show between nine and twelve months.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in West Jordan

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. Most sexual side effects go away after stopping, and some fade on their own. Mood changes are rare but should be reported promptly. Scalp itch is the usual topical complaint. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. Expect questions about mood history, libido and family planning, because each can change the plan. PSA readings drop on finasteride. Tell any doctor who checks your prostate that you take it.

    How Men's Options Compare in West Jordan

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. Dutasteride with minoxidil is a stronger DHT-blocking option, used off-label, for men who did not get enough from finasteride after about a year. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a topical-only blend for men who want to skip DHT blockers. Transplants help some men.
