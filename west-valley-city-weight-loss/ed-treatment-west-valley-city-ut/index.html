    How ED Medication Works in West Valley City

    An erection depends on blood flowing into the penis faster than it drains out, and the enzyme PDE5 is what normally ends that process once arousal fades. Sildenafil and tadalafil block PDE5, so blood flow holds longer. Arousal still has to happen. These pills do not raise desire on their own, and both are FDA-approved for ED.

    Who ED Treatment Is For

    ED treatment is for adult men who regularly have trouble getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex, whether it started gradually over years or showed up after a stressful stretch. ED becomes more common after 40 and with diabetes or high blood pressure. Younger men get it too. Performance anxiety and heavy drinking are common causes in men under 40, and a low dose can help.

    Dosing and Schedule in West Valley City

    Sildenafil is usually taken 30 to 60 minutes before sex in 25, 50 or 100 mg doses, and tadalafil as needed comes in 10 or 20 mg doses that can work for up to 36 hours. A low daily tadalafil dose of 2.5 or 5 mg skips the timing entirely. One dose per day, maximum. Your provider adjusts the strength after your first few tries.

    Many men notice a difference the first time, but the honest advice from most providers is to give a new medication at least 4 to 6 tries before judging it, since nerves often get in the way early. Timing and food make a big difference with sildenafil. Track what works. If you are on daily tadalafil, expect steady effect after roughly 5 days.
